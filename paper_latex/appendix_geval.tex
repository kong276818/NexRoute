\section{G-Eval Preliminary Quality Assessment}
\label{appendix:geval}

Table~\ref{tab:geval_preliminary} reports G-Eval quality assessment scores
for all 7B- and 72B-backbone models on CROSS-domain queries.
Evaluation was conducted using Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct as an independent judge
over 50 sampled CROSS-domain responses per model, scoring four criteria on a
1--5 integer scale: Relevance, Coherence, Factuality, and Domain Expertise.
These results are retained as a preliminary reference; the ceiling effect
observed in subsequent Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct evaluations ($n{=}200$,
summarised in Section~\ref{sec:factuality}) precluded extension to a larger
evaluation set.
