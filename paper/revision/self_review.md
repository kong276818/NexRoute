**심사자 비판 (예상)**:
> "The paper proposes a dual-axis routing framework combining domain and function classification. **한 단락 대응** (논문 Related Work 마지막 단락에 명시):
> NexRoute differs from MoE and LoRA-MoE architectures in three structural ways that are absent from all prior systems simultaneously: (1) routing decisions are bound to retrieval scope (domain-exclusive partitions), not just to expert selection; (2) a second, independent routing axis explicitly determines reasoning format (prompt scaffold); (3) both decisions are made from a shared encoder in one forward pass, ensuring context-prompt alignment. Critically, the central empirical finding---that oracle-domain routing (accuracy upper bound) degrades quality by 1.72pp---demonstrates that routing probability optimization is not the productive design choice. No prior MoE system has made this structural claim or provided this evidence.

### Reject 사유 5: "Benchmark circularity: gold answers and corpus are both from Qwen2.5-72B"

**심사자 비판 (예상)**:
> "The evaluation corpus (MMLU rewritten), gold answers, and synthesis data for the CROSS adapter are all generated by Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct. The NexRoute backbone (Qwen2.5-7B) is from the same model family. This creates potential stylistic circularity in all lexical metrics (ROUGE-L, BERTScore), making it impossible to determine whether quality gains reflect genuine expert system improvement or stylistic alignment with the gold generator."

**대응**:
> This concern is acknowledged and partially mitigated: (1) BERTScore uses RoBERTa-large (non-Qwen encoder) to reduce embedding-space alignment; (2) NLI contradiction rate uses an independent cross-encoder (nli-deberta-v3-large) as the factuality proxy; (3) External benchmarks (MedQA, MMLU-Law) use gold answers from the official benchmarks, not Qwen-generated. The circularity risk affects absolute metric levels but not relative comparisons between systems, as all baselines use the same gold answers. We are explicit about this in Section 7.1 (Limitations).

**원고 반영**: External benchmark 결과 채우면 이 concern의 일부가 해소됨.

## 최종 체크: "이거 기존 MoE랑 뭐가 다른데?" 한 단락 답변

**Q: NexRoute와 기존 MoE의 차이가 무엇인가?**

A: MoE systems select which expert to activate; NexRoute additionally determines what the activated expert receives (domain-exclusive evidence partition) and how the prompt is structured (function-conditioned scaffold). The distinction is not architectural complexity but empirical principle: our oracle routing experiment shows that routing accuracy optimization---the objective all existing MoE systems optimize---is not the productive design choice. Increasing routing accuracy to its oracle upper bound *decreases* generation quality by 1.72pp (p<0.001). This finding, absent from all prior MoE work, reframes the design objective: structural wiring between routing decisions and downstream system resources, not routing probability optimization, is what drives expert system quality.
